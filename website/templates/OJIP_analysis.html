Welcome to the tool analyzing fast chlorophyll fluorescence induction kinetics! Fast chlorophyll fluorescence induction kinetics, known also as OJIP transient, provides a rapid and sensitive method for assessing the efficiency of electron (e-) transport within the photosynthetic electron transport chain. Analysis of the OJIP transient allows to determine bottlenecks in the e- transport chain at various sites — including charge separation at photosystem II (PSII), the plastoquinon (PQ) pool, or at the site of photosystem I (PSI).

Spline fitting quality (kr)
About fitting methods

Spline methods reconstruct the full transient with a spline and compute D2/D3 derivatives. FJ/FI are detected from D2 troughs:

Standard spline
Linear LSQ spline (kr adjustable). FJ/FI from D2 troughs.
Log-time spline
Quintic spline in log-time (smooth D2/D3). FJ/FI from D2 troughs.
PCHIP
Monotone piecewise cubic (no tunable params). FJ/FI from D2 troughs.

Detection methods use log-time spline for reconstruction (D2/D3/residuals) but detect FJ/FI with a separate algorithm:

Polynomial (Akinyemi)
FJ/FI from a 9th-degree polynomial fitted in narrow windows (0.5–5 ms for J, 9–100 ms for I). Spline-independent → more reproducible. Ref: Akinyemi et al. 2023, Photosynth. Res.
D1 local minima
FJ/FI from local minima of the 1st derivative D1 = dV/d(log t), i.e. plateaus where the fluorescence rise rate is slowest. Mirrors the D2 trough approach but in D1 space. FP uses D2 trough fallback.
3-Exp decomposition
Fits V(t) = Σ Ai(1−e−t/τi) with 3 exponential components (O-J, J-I, I-P). FJ/FI from rate crossovers between adjacent components. Ref: Boisvert et al. 2006, FEBS J.
Piecewise-linear
Fits 4 linear segments in V vs log10(t) with 3 optimized breakpoints. FJ/FI/FP directly from breakpoint positions. Robust, no derivative noise.
Gaussian D1
Deconvolves D1 into 2–3 Gaussian peaks in log10(t) space. FJ/FI from valleys (local minima) between adjacent Gaussian centers. Intuitive spectral decomposition of the fluorescence rise.

FP always uses D2 trough for spline and most detection methods. Piecewise-linear derives all three breakpoints directly from the fit.
